5. Member Greetings by Text Message and Email

Program name: Mizpah Lodge #302 Member Greetings.

Description: Members who opt in receive a greeting from Mizpah Lodge #302 on their birthday and on their Masonic anniversary. The messages are personal greetings only; they do not contain advertising or requests for money.

How to opt in: by indicating on your membership information, or by telling the Lodge Secretary, that you agree to receive birthday and anniversary greetings by text message and/or email at the mobile number or email address you provide. Text message and email consent are recorded separately. Consent is not a condition of membership or of any purchase. The verbal script is on our Member Greetings Opt-In page, and the printable consent form is also online.

Message frequency: about two messages per year (one for your birthday and one for your Masonic anniversary), plus occasional service messages, such as a confirmation when you opt out.

Cost: the Lodge does not charge for these messages. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

Opting out: reply STOP to any text message to stop all text messages. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out. To stop email greetings, reply to any greeting email or contact the Secretary. You can opt back in by contacting the Secretary.

Help: reply HELP to any text message, or contact the Lodge Secretary at Mizpah302sec@gmail.com or (402) 558-0666.

Carriers: wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Text messages are sent only to U.S. mobile numbers.

Privacy: your mobile number and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing. See section 4 of our Privacy Policy.

Please let the Secretary know if your mobile number or email address changes, so greetings do not go to someone else.
